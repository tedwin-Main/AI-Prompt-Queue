AI Prompt Queue - Setup and Usage Guide

INSTALLATION
1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Go to chrome://extensions/
3. Turn on "Developer mode" in the top right.
4. Click "Load unpacked" and select the "extension" folder located at `/Users/sloth/AI Prompt Queue/extension`.
5. Pin the extension to your toolbar for easy access.

USAGE
1. Go to gemini.google.com and log in.
2. Open the extension popup.
3. Queue prompts manually, import a .txt file (one prompt per line block, separated by double newlines), or select text on any page and right click -> "Add to Queue".
4. Adjust the delay slider if needed (default is 3 seconds).
5. Press "Start". The extension will automatically type and send each prompt, wait for Gemini to finish generating, save the result, and move to the next.
6. You can "Pause" at any time and "Resume" later.
7. If Gemini hits a rate limit, the queue will automatically pause and alert you.

HISTORY & EXPORT
1. Click the "History" tab to view all completed prompts and their corresponding responses.
2. Click "Export Results" to download a .json file of your entire queue history.
3. Click "Clear" to wipe the history.
